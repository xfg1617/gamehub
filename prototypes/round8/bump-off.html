BUMP OFF · PROTOTYPE
Everyone plans in secret, then everyone moves at once. Knock the others off the pond. Last boat floating wins the round, first to 3 rounds wins.

2 · REVEALEveryone appears with their arrow. Now you see who is coming for you, but it's too late to change!

Tips: aim where you think someone will be, not where they are. A small move to dodge can be as good as a big hit. The pond gets smaller every turn after the 2nd.
