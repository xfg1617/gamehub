Case #001 · The Harbor Pearl

How the case is meant to be solved, step by step, and which partner holds each piece. Each clue is tagged A · SCENE, B · RECORDS or INTERVIEW (Detective A asks the questions).

Answer: Marcus Bell · took the spare key from the housekeeping cart and came in through the garden door during the blackout · because he owed $12,000 to someone called Ricky.

The idea behind the case

Each partner can see only half of the answer. Detective A can prove someone in room 204 walked through the mud, and Detective B can prove Marcus lied about never leaving his room. Neither of you can close the case alone: you have to put the two halves together on the board.

Step by step

1

When did it happen?

A The display case's alarm "runs on main power". B The power company notice says the power went out at 9:20 pm, and the generator log says the lights came back at 9:34 pm.

So the theft happened in the dark window, between 9:20 and 9:34. Daisy's sighting (about 9:25) fits right in the middle.

2

How was the case opened?

A No scratches, no broken glass: it was opened with its key.

Victor says there are only two keys: his (always on his belt) and a spare on the housekeeping key ring.

A The housekeeping cart on floor 2 has an empty LIB-C hook. B Lily's log: she left the cart outside room 204 at 9:10, and found the key missing at 9:50.

So the thief took the spare key from the cart between 9:10 and the theft. The cart was parked at the quiet end of floor 2, next to room 204 and the back stairs.

3

Which way did the thief go?

A Wet size 11 zigzag sneaker prints go from the library's garden door to the case and back. The garden door never shuts properly. Outside, the same prints run along the garden path to the back staircase up to floor 2.

Daisy saw "someone tall in a hooded jacket, with a phone light" on that path around 9:25, going toward the library and later back toward the stairs.

So the thief came from floor 2, by the back stairs, and went back up the same way.

5

Catch Marcus's lie

B The floor 2 door log: room 204 was opened from inside at 9:18 pm and opened with Marcus's card at 9:43 pm. He left two minutes before the blackout and came back after the lights were on.

A In his closet: size 11 zigzag sneakers, still wet and muddy, plus a damp hooded jacket. They match the prints in the library and on the path, and the "tall figure in a hood" Daisy saw.

Confronting him (once the door log is on the board): "Okay, I stepped out for some air." He has now changed his story.

6

Why did he do it?

B Phone records: at 8:47 pm "RICKY $$" texts "12k by Monday. Last warning." At 9:44 pm, one minute after getting back to his room, Marcus replies "Relax. You'll have it tomorrow."

A On his desk: a pawn shop card, "Sat 9:00 AM. Brooch. Bring it."

B Mrs. Bell: Marcus asked her for money again and she said no.

Motive: he needed money fast to pay a debt.

The strongest evidence to choose

When accusing, you pick 3 pieces of evidence. Any 3 of these count as strong (5 stars if no hints or wrong guesses):

  • Floor 2 door log B: proves he lied and was out during the blackout.
  • Sneakers in room 204 A and Wet sneaker prints A: put his shoes in the library.
  • Housekeeping cart A / Housekeeping log B: the missing spare key, right outside his door.
  • Prints on the garden path A: the route back up to floor 2.
  • Pawn shop card A and Marcus's phone records B: the motive.

The best combination ties both partners together: door log + sneakers + phone records.

What really happened

8:44 pm Marcus goes up to room 204, saying he has a headache. 8:47 Ricky's threat arrives.

9:10 Lily parks her cart outside 204 and goes down to the laundry. Marcus notices the spare keys.

9:18 He slips out and takes the LIB-C key. 9:20 the storm kills the power and the case alarm.

~9:25 In his hooded jacket, using his phone light, he goes down the back stairs, along the muddy path and in through the library door that never shuts. Daisy, under the awning, sees a tall figure.

~9:27 He opens the case with the key, takes the Harbor Pearl and goes back the same way.

9:34 Victor gets the generator running. 9:43 Marcus is back in 204. 9:44 "You'll have it tomorrow."

9:50 Lily finds the key missing. 9:58 Mrs. Bell finds the empty case. The brooch is later found sewn into the lining of Marcus's jacket.

Where the two partners need to talk

  • A finds the empty key hook; only B's housekeeping log says when the cart was left there and that Lily was away.
  • B sees Marcus's door opened at 9:18 and 9:43; only A can find the wet sneakers that show where he went.
  • Ethan looks guilty to A (the doodles); only B's 9:28 receipt clears him.
  • Victor has a key; A hears Chef Bao's alibi, and B has the generator log that backs it up.
  • A can only confront Marcus with the door log and phone records after B pins them to the board.

Fairness check
